Before we begin

A few good
questions.

Every space is different. A consultation is the place to ask questions and work through the details.

What should I include in a consultation request?

Tell us which spaces or surfaces you have in mind, your general location and your ideal timing. Photos, approximate dimensions and any color ideas can help shape the conversation.

Can we talk through color and finish options?

Yes. We’ll discuss the feeling you want and suggest samples to try in your own light.

What preparation might my surfaces need?

It depends on the surface and its condition. We’ll review this together before work begins.

How should I prepare my space for painting?

We’ll share simple steps for clearing and protecting the area when we define the plan.

What affects the project schedule and cost?

Scope, surface condition, preparation, finish choices and timing all play a part.

FAQ

Common
questions.

Answers to common questions before you request a consultation.

What should I include in a consultation request?

Include the spaces or surfaces, your location and your timing. Photos and approximate dimensions help.

Can you help with color and finish?

Yes. We’ll recommend options and samples to test in your space.

What preparation might my surfaces need?

It depends on the surface and its condition. We’ll confirm it before work begins.

How should I prepare my space for painting?

We’ll give you clear instructions for clearing and protecting the area.

What affects the project schedule and cost?

Scope, surface condition, preparation, finishes and timing.
